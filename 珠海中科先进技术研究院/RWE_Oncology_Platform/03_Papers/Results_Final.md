## Patient Characteristics

A total of **320** patients with R/R DLBCL were included, of whom **132 (41.3%)** received PD-1-based immunotherapy.

Before weighting, patients in the PD-1 group had slightly better performance status and lower LDH levels, suggesting potential treatment selection bias. After stabilized IPTW with trimming, all baseline covariates were well balanced, with standardized mean differences reduced to **<0.10**.

The effective sample size (ESS) after weighting was **258.7 (80.8%)**.

---

## Effectiveness of PD-1-Based Therapy

In the IPTW-weighted Cox model, PD-1-based therapy was associated with improved overall survival (**HR 0.69, 95% CI 0.52–0.92; p = 0.011**).

Weighted Kaplan-Meier curves demonstrated clear separation favoring the PD-1 group, with longer median survival.

---

## Impact of Immune-Related Adverse Events

A total of **78 patients (24.4%)** experienced at least one irAE.

In the time-dependent Cox model, irAE occurrence was associated with improved overall survival (**HR 0.63, 95% CI 0.45–0.88; p = 0.006**).

---

## Interaction Analysis

A significant interaction was observed between PD-1 therapy and irAE occurrence (**p for interaction = 0.028**).

Among PD-1-treated patients, those who developed irAE had substantially improved survival (**HR 0.57, 95% CI 0.37–0.86**).

---

## Sensitivity Analyses

Results remained consistent across different trimming thresholds (1%-99% vs 5%-95%) and landmark analyses (3-month, 6-month).

E-value analysis indicated that unmeasured confounding would need to be very strong (>2.5) to explain away the observed associations.
