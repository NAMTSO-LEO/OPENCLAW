## 🎯 压力测试核心原则

Director压力面试不是考你"知道多少"，而是考你：
1. 优先级判断力

## Q1: Why should this platform be funded? (为什么要投这个平台?)

### 压力点
- 让你从"技术价值"切换到"业务价值"
- 考验你是否知道"不买会怎样"

### 回答框架

> "Three reasons why funding this is a business decision, not a technology request:
> 
> First, without this platform, every evidence generation project will continue to take weeks to months longer than necessary—because we're reinventing standards each time. That's an invisible tax on every study.
> 
> Second, our regulatory readiness is inconsistent. Some studies are audit-ready, others aren't—depending on who runs them. This platform standardizes that, so we enter regulatory discussions with confidence.
> 
> Third, competitive landscape is shifting. Other organizations are building these capabilities. The one who standardizes first will have faster evidence cycles. This is a competitive advantage investment, not a cost."

## Q2: What would you deprioritize? (你会优先级什么?)

### 压力点
- 测试你是否知道"不做什么"
- 避免"什么都想做"

### 回答框架

> "If I had to deprioritize something, it would be advanced ML prediction before foundational evidence credibility is established.
> 
> Here's my logic: AI prediction is valuable, but it depends on data quality and methodological rigor. If we haven't proven our bias-control methods and evidence packaging, adding ML layers creates a 'garbage in, garbage out' problem—except with more complexity.
> 
> So the priority is: first establish the bias-control engine and evidence package as reliable. Then layer on prediction as an extension, not the foundation.
> 
> This is exactly the trade-off I'm comfortable with: pause the shiny layer, protect the foundational layer."

## Q3: How do you scale across teams? (如何扩展到多个团队?)

### 压力点
- 不是"做不做"，而是"怎么规模化"
- 考验组织运营能力

### 回答框架

> "Scale happens through three mechanisms, not one:
> 
> First, **standardization at the foundation**. Data tiers, analytical standards, evidence templates—these are not optional. They apply to every team, every study. Consistency is non-negotiable.
> 
> Second, **flexibility at the interpretation layer**. Teams can customize subgroup analyses, novel method extensions, study-specific hypotheses—where expert scientific judgment matters. We protect that flexibility.
> 
> Third, **knowledge transfer through training and governance**. Not just 'here's the code'—but 'here's why this design works, here's how to interpret results, here's when to escalate.' This builds internal capability, not dependency.
> 
> My goal is: when I leave the room, the platform should still work. That means institutionalization, not just documentation."

## Q4: What is your leadership model? (你的领导力模型是什么?)

### 压力点
- 不是"你做什么"，而是"你怎么带团队"
- 区分IC和Director的关键问题

### 回答框架

> "My leadership model has three pillars:
> 
> **Pillar 1: Define the framework, not just the task.**
> I don't just assign analyses—I define what good looks like, what standards are non-negotiable, what the evidence package should contain. People know what to aim for.
> 
> **Pillar 2: Align stakeholders, not just manage tasks.**
> This platform requires alignment across statistics, medical, regulatory, and operations. My role isn't to do the work—it's to make sure everyone is pulling in the same direction, with the same standards.
> 
> **Pillar 3: Institutionalize, don't just deliver.**
> A project has an end. A capability has a lifecycle. I think about how this platform lives beyond any single study or person. That's why I focus on standards, governance, and training—not just outputs.
> 
> In short: I lead through framework, alignment, and institutionalization."

## Q5: How do you handle conflict between speed and rigor? (如何平衡速度与严谨?)

### 压力点
- 常见管理困境
- 测试实际判断力

### 回答框架

> "This is the tension I navigate every day. Here's my principle:
> 
> **Different evidence questions warrant different rigor levels.**
> 
> If we're doing exploratory signal detection—quick hypothesis generation, early decision support—faster methods with acknowledged limitations are acceptable.
> 
> If the evidence may influence a development decision, regulatory submission, or safety signal—then I insist on higher rigor: bias diagnostics, sensitivity analysis, transparent assumptions.
> 
> I never compromise on rigor for speed. But I do calibrate rigor to the decision context. That's the judgment I bring as a leader.
> 
> And I make this explicit to stakeholders: 'For this question, here's what we can say with confidence. For that question, here's what we need more time to validate.' Transparency builds trust."

## Q6: What's your biggest failure in building this platform? (最大失败是什么?)

### 压力点
- 测试自我认知和反思能力
- Director必须能坦诚自己的问题

### 回答框架

> "My biggest learning was underestimating change management.
> 
> I built the technical framework first, then tried to get adoption. That's backwards. People don't adopt systems—they adopt solutions to their pain.
> 
> What I should have done: started with one high-visibility use case, demonstrated value, then expanded. Build the case, not just the platform.
> 
> If I could redo it, I'd spend more time with stakeholders upfront—understanding what they need, what their pressure points are, how this helps their specific problems. Technical excellence without adoption is just academic exercise."

## Q7: How do you measure success? (如何衡量成功?)

### 压力点
- 测试你是否知道"成功长什么样"
- 是否有量化思维

### 回答框架

> "I measure success across four dimensions:
> 
> **Time-to-evidence**: Can we generate decision-ready evidence faster than before? Target: 30% reduction in project initiation time.
> 
> **Cross-study reusability**: How much of the framework can be reused? Target: 70% of data standards and 50% of methods reusable across new studies.
> 
> **Regulatory readiness**: How often do we enter regulatory discussions without major methodological gaps? Target: 90% of relevant studies audit-ready.
> 
> **Organizational capability**: How many team members can execute without heavy依赖 on me? Target: At least 5 people trained on core framework by end of year.
> 
> These aren't just metrics—they're accountabilities."

## Q8: What would make you quit this platform? (什么时候会放弃?)

### 压力点
- 测试是否有"止损思维"
- 能否理性判断

### 回答框架

> "I would recommend pausing or pivoting if:
> 
> First, **data quality consistently fails the fit-for-purpose test**. If we can't get Tier 1 or Tier 2 data for key use cases, the platform becomes a theoretical exercise. Evidence quality depends on input quality.
> 
> Second, **regulatory landscape shifts significantly**. If agencies suddenly require RCT-only for all decisions, the RWE value proposition changes. We'd need to adapt.
> 
> Third, **stakeholder alignment breaks down**. If clinical, regulatory, and business can't agree on what evidence is needed, we build a platform nobody uses.
> 
> But absent those three, I believe in the approach. The need is real. The methods are sound. The platform is the right investment."

## Q9: How do you handle a team member who resists standardization?

### 压力点
- 测试实际团队管理能力
- 不是理论，是现实问题

### 回答框架

> "This happens. My approach is three-fold:
> 
> **First, understand the resistance.** Sometimes it's legitimate—they see a constraint that breaks a valid scientific workflow. I listen.
> 
> **Second, clarify the difference between standardization and rigidity.** Standardization is about consistency and auditability. It doesn't prevent scientific judgment—it protects it. I explain: 'Your analysis can still be novel; your data qualification still needs to be rigorous.'
> 
> **Third, make the case, not the mandate.** I don't enforce compliance—I make the business case. 'Without this standard, your results can't be compared to other studies, can't support cross-indication decisions, can't enter regulatory discussions confidently.' Most professionals respond to that logic.
> 
> If someone fundamentally resists for non-scientific reasons, that's a management issue beyond this platform."

## Q10: What do you need from leadership to make this succeed?

### 压力点
- 测试你是否知道"成功需要什么资源"
- 不是伸手要，是理性规划

### 回答框架

> "Three things I need:
> 
> **1. Commitment to standards over convenience.**
> Leadership needs to signal that consistency matters, even when it's faster to do things 'our own way.' Without that signal, standardization becomes optional.
> 
> **2. Protected time for platform work.**
> This can't be a side project. I need dedicated capacity—not just me, but at least one other person—to maintain the framework, update standards, train new team members.
> 
> **3. Cross-functional sponsorship.**
> This platform touches statistics, medical, regulatory, operations. I need leadership to signal that this is a priority across those functions—not just in our team.
> 
> Give me those three, and I'll deliver the platform. Without them, it becomes another initiative that fades."
