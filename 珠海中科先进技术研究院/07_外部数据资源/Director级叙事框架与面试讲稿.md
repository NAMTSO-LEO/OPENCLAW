## 四、完整讲稿版本

### 30秒版

I lead the development of an AI-driven RWE platform for oncology that turns heterogeneous real-world data into regulatory-aligned evidence. The key is not just advanced analytics, but building a reusable system that standardizes data, controls bias, and supports drug development decisions at scale.

---

### 2分钟版

In oncology, traditional trials remain essential, but they are often too slow, too costly, and not always sufficient for the full range of evidence needs across development and post-marketing. At the same time, real-world data is abundant but difficult to use reliably because of heterogeneity, bias, and limited standardization.

My approach has been to build a reusable RWE platform rather than solve each study as a one-off analysis. The platform starts with fit-for-purpose data, using tiered source evaluation and CDISC-aligned standardization. On top of that, we apply a bias-control framework that combines target trial emulation, IPTW or doubly robust adjustment, and time-dependent survival methods when needed.

The output is not just statistical analysis, but decision-ready evidence packages—hazard ratios, survival curves, sensitivity analyses, and explainable prediction outputs—that can support effectiveness evaluation, safety monitoring, and patient stratification.

What makes the platform valuable is scale: once the data standards and analytical patterns are established, the same framework can be reused across multiple studies and programs, improving consistency, speed, and regulatory readiness.

## 五、Director级Q&A全面解析

### Q1: 你的平台和普通RWE分析有什么不同？

**核心答法**: "The difference is that I treat RWE as a system capability, not an individual study output."

---

### Q2: 为什么不能一个项目一个项目做？

**答法**: "That approach does not scale. It creates duplication, inconsistency, and high dependence on individual analysts."

---

### Q3: 你怎么决定先做哪些use case？

**答法**: "I prioritize based on clinical relevance, regulatory potential, and reusability of methods."

---

### Q4: 你如何确保科学严谨？

**答法**: "I separate rigor into three layers: data fitness, design validity, and analytical diagnostics."

---

### Q5: 你如何处理confounding？

**答法**: "I treat confounding as a design and estimation problem, not just a modeling problem."

---

### Q6: 如何处理immortal time bias？

**答法**: "Whenever exposure or events evolve over time, I avoid static baseline treatment assumptions and use time-dependent structures or landmark approaches."

---

### Q7: 如何保证监管可接受性？

**答法**: "Regulatory readiness comes from traceability, fit-for-purpose data, transparent assumptions, and methods aligned with the evidence question."

---

### Q8: 你的角色是什么？

**答法**: "My role is to define the framework, align stakeholders around standards, prioritize scalable use cases, and ensure methodological consistency."

---

### Q9: 怎么带团队？

**答法**: "I lead through frameworks, standards, and coaching. Rather than reviewing only outputs, I make sure teams understand why a design is chosen."

---

### Q10: 业务方只想要结果，不想要严格方法怎么办？

**答法**: "My role is to find the right balance: move quickly where exploratory, but insist on higher rigor when the result may influence development, regulatory, or safety decisions."

---

### Q11: 你怎么看AI在这个平台里的位置？

**答法**: "I see AI as an accelerator and extension layer, not a substitute for study design."

---

### Q12: 如何scale到更多适应症？

**答法**: "The point of standardizing tiers, data mapping, and analytical patterns is that the core framework becomes reusable."
