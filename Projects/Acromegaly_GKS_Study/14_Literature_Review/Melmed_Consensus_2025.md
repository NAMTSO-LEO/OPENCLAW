### Overview

This is the **authoritative consensus statement** from 52 worldwide experts updating acromegaly management guidelines. Critical for defining remission criteria and treatment algorithm.

---

### Key Consensus Definitions (Box 1)

#### Biochemical Criteria

| Parameter | Threshold | Recommendation |
|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| **GH nadir (OGTT)** | <1 ng/mL (traditional) | Strong recommendation |
| **GH nadir (ultrasensitive assay)** | <0.4 ng/mL | Discretionary recommendation |
| **IGF-1** | Within age-stratified normal range | Strong recommendation |
| **Post-op measurement timing** | 3 months after surgery | Strong recommendation |
| **Follow-up duration** | ≥5 years | Strong recommendation |

#### Imaging Criteria

| Parameter | Recommendation |
|-----------|---------------|
| **First post-op MRI** | ≥3 months after surgery (to allow involution) |
| **Routine MRI** | Not indicated for all - based on biochemical/clinical features |
| **MRI during medical therapy** | 6 months after initiation/switch |
| **PET (Methionine)** | Useful when MRI negative but GH hypersecretion persists |

#### Molecular/Pathological

| Factor | Recommendation |
|--------|---------------|
| **Granulation pattern** | Minimum requirement for SRL responsiveness |
| **SSTR2 immunostaining** | May predict postoperative SRL responsiveness |
| **GNAS mutations** | Do NOT predict SRL responsiveness (not included in algorithm) |

---

### Treatment Algorithm Summary

```
First-line: Surgery (by experienced pituitary surgeon)
           ↓ (if not controlled)
First-line Medical: Somatostatin Receptor Ligands (SRLs)
           ↓ (if not controlled)
Second-line: ↑ SRL dose/frequency OR Add cabergoline OR Add pegvisomant
           ↓ (if still not controlled)
Third-line: Combination therapy (SRL + pegvisomant) OR Radiation therapy
```

---

### Key Medical Therapy Data

| Treatment | Biochemical Control Rate | Notes |
|-----------|--------------------------|-------|
| **SRLs (first-line)** | ~40% overall | Not all achieve "clinical disease control" |
| **Pegvisomant** | High (IGF-1 normalization) | Monitor liver function, no routine MRI needed |
| **Cabergoline** | Modest | Only for IGF-1 <2.0-2.5×ULN or mixed GH-PRL adenomas |
| **Combination SRL + Pegvisomant** | Higher | Useful for partial SRL responders |

### Critical Consensus Points for Your Paper

#### 1. Terminology
> "As the term 'cure', defined as an absolute return to the premorbid condition, is seldom achieved, the term 'disease control' on medical treatment or 'remission' after surgery or radiotherapy is the preferred management goal"

**Your implication:** Use "remission" not "cure" for GKS outcomes

#### 2. GH Cut-off Update
> "GH nadir levels <1 ng/mL during OGTT reflect postoperative long-term remission. However, when using modern ultrasensitive GH assays with OGTT, a GH cut-off of 0.4 ng/ml is recommended"

**Your implication:** Specify your assay type and justify cut-off

#### 3. Imaging Timing
> "The first follow-up MRI should be performed no sooner than 3 months after surgery"

**Your implication:** For GKS, similar timing considerations apply
