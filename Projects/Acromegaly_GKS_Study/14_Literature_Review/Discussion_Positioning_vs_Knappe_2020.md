## Suggested Discussion Paragraph

### Comparison with Radiotherapy Outcomes

The German Acromegaly Registry provides the largest direct comparison of fractionated radiotherapy versus stereotactic radiosurgery for acromegaly. In 352 patients with over 4,000 person-years of observation, the 10-year remission rates were 48% for FRT and 52% for SRS, with no statistically significant difference (p=0.74).

Importantly, SRS achieved disease control faster (mean 2.1 years vs 3.0 years, p=0.021) and was associated with lower odds of new pituitary insufficiency (OR=0.54, p=0.049). These findings support the growing preference for SRS in appropriate candidates.

Our study builds upon this registry data by specifically examining Gamma Knife radiosurgery as either primary or adjuvant treatment, with particular focus on cavernous sinus-invading disease. While the registry represents real-world practice across multiple centers, our study applies more rigorous analytical approaches including overlap weighting to address treatment selection bias.
