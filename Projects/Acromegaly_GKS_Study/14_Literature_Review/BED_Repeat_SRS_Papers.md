# Literature Review: BED and Repeat SRS Papers (16-17)

## Article 16: BED Analysis

---

### Publication Details

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Title** | BED (Biological Effective Dose) analysis for GKRS |
| **Key Finding** | BED > 170 Gy predicts better remission |
| **Important Insight** | At fixed dose (25 Gy), BED not significant - biology dominates |

---

### Key Results

| Factor | Effect | P-value |
|--------|--------|---------|
| **BED > 170** | HR ~2.0 remission | Significant |
| **IGF-1i > 1.5** | Lower remission | Significant |
| **At fixed 25 Gy** | BED NS, IGF-1 significant | Important |

**Key Insight**: When dose range narrows, biology (IGF-1) regains dominance

### Toxicity

- Hypopituitarism: 36.8% (3yr), 53.2% (5yr)
- **BED NOT predictive of toxicity**

## Ready-to-Use Discussion Paragraph

> "The effectiveness of Gamma Knife radiosurgery in acromegaly is governed by a complex interaction between tumor biology, anatomical constraints, and biologically effective dose, with outcomes evolving over time and fundamentally differing between primary and salvage treatment settings. When the dose range is constrained, biological factors such as baseline IGF-1 regain predictive prominence, suggesting a ceiling effect of radiation dose beyond which additional biological targeting may be required."
