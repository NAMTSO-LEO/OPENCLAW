### Toxicity

- New hormone deficiency: 31.6%
- Panhypopituitarism: 1.5%

- Margin dose ≥25 Gy (HR 2.68, p=0.021)
- Tumor volume ≥2.5 mL (HR 2.22, p=0.025)

## Final Framework: Complete GKRS Model for Acromegaly

### Summary of Key Findings

1. **Baseline Biology** (IGF-1, GH): Consistently strongest predictor
2. **Anatomy** (CS invasion): Affects both surgery and radiation
3. **Radiation Physics** (BED, margin dose): Modifies speed of response
4. **Time**: Central variable - remission is cumulative, toxicity is cumulative
5. **Early Response** (6-month GH change): New strongest predictor
6. **Treatment History** (primary vs salvage): Different dynamics

### Remission Evolution

- 2 years: ~30-40%
- 4 years: ~60-65%
- 8 years: ~75-80%
- 10+ years: ~60-70% (with some late recurrences)

### Toxicity Evolution

- Hypopituitarism: ~20-30% by 5 years, ~40-50% by 10 years
- Visual toxicity: <5% (very low with modern planning)
- Stroke risk: No increased risk (long-term data)
