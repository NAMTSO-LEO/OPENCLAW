### Study Overview

This is a large surgical cohort (N=401) evaluating endoscopic transsphenoidal surgery outcomes for acromegaly. While it focuses on surgery (not GKS), it provides critical data on cavernous sinus invasion impact that is directly relevant to your study.

---

### Key Results

#### Overall Remission Rates

| Category | N | Remission Rate |
|----------|---|----------------|
| **Overall** | 401 | 68.1% (273/401) |
| Microadenomas | 107 | **81.3%** |
| Macroadenomas | 294 | 63.3% |
| **Cavernous sinus invasion** | 86 | **40.7%** |
| Bilateral CS invasion | 15 | 26.7% |

#### Preoperative GH Levels & Remission

| Pre-op GH (ng/mL) | N | Remission Rate |
|-------------------|---|----------------|
| <20 | 273 | 72.4% |
| 20-40 | 66 | - |
| 40-60 | 23 | - |
| >60 | 29 | **37.9%** |

---

### Key Predictors (Correlation Analysis)

| Factor | Correlation (r) | P-value |
|--------|-----------------|---------|
| **Cavernous sinus invasion** | -0.953 | <0.001 (very strong) |
| **Extrapseudocapsular resection** | 0.810 | <0.001 (very strong) |
| Adenoma size/volume | Weak, inverse | Significant |

---

### Key Findings

#### 1. Cavernous Sinus Invasion = Most Important Negative Factor

> "There was, conversely, a very strongly significant correlation with cavernous sinus invasion (P < 0.001; r: -0.953)"

Remission rate drops from 68.1% overall to **40.7%** with CS invasion.

#### 2. Extrapseudocapsular Resection = Key Positive Factor

> "remission was very strongly significantly correlated with extrapseudocapsular resection (P < 0.001; r: 0.810)"

75% remission rate with pseudocapsular resection (n=28).

#### 3. Tumor Size Matters

- Microadenoma: 81.3% remission
- Macroadenoma: 63.3% remission
- CS invasion: 40.7% remission

---

### Surgical Technique Notes

- Pure endoscopic endonasal approach
- Neuronavigation used
- Transsellar, transethmoidal, transmaxillary approaches for CS invasion
- Median follow-up: 13-182 months

---

### Remission Criteria (2010 Consensus)

- IGF-1 normal for age and sex
- Random GH <1 ng/mL OR GH nadir <0.4 ng/mL after OGTT
