This study reports the largest experience with fractionated intensity-modulated radiotherapy (IMRT) for acromegaly, providing direct data on efficacy and safety of modern radiotherapy techniques.

### Key Findings

#### 1. IMRT Achieves High Long-Term Remission

- 5-year remission rate: 74.3%
- Similar to SRS outcomes
- Slower initial response but durable

#### 2. IGF-1i is Key Predictor

- IGF-1i <2.5 → better remission
- Effect remains significant in multivariate

#### 3. Younger Age Predicts Both Remission and Toxicity

- Age <33: better remission
- Age <33: more hypopituitarism (paradox)

#### 4. SIB-IMRT Increases Toxicity

- Associated with higher hypopituitarism (p=0.017)
- Higher dose to larger volume may explain this
