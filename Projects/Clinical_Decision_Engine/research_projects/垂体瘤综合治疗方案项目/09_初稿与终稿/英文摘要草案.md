# English Abstract Draft

**Title:** Integrating Molecular Target Screening, Gamma Knife Imaging, and Artificial Intelligence for Precision Management of Pituitary Tumors: A Translational Research Plan

**Abstract:**
Pituitary tumors remain clinically challenging because of postoperative residue, recurrence, heterogeneous response to medical therapy, and variable outcomes after Gamma Knife radiosurgery. To address these unmet needs, we propose a translational research framework that integrates molecular target screening, MRI/Gamma Knife imaging features, and artificial intelligence/machine learning (AI/ML) modeling for risk stratification and treatment optimization. Candidate molecular markers, including Ki-67, p53, MGMT, SSTR2/SSTR5, dopamine D2 receptor, USP8, and pathway-related biomarkers, will be combined with clinical variables, radiological characteristics, radiomics features, and Gamma Knife dosimetric parameters. Multi-step feature selection approaches such as LASSO, Elastic Net, Random Forest importance ranking, XGBoost, and SHAP-based interpretability analyses will be used to identify robust predictive factors associated with local control failure, recurrence, and biochemical non-remission. We further propose the development of multimodal predictive models and comparison of molecular-only, imaging-only, and integrated models, with internal and external validation whenever feasible. The ultimate goal is to establish an interpretable and clinically actionable decision-support framework that may guide individualized Gamma Knife timing, follow-up intensity, and precision treatment strategies for pituitary tumors. If supported by rigorous validation and clinically meaningful endpoints, this approach may provide a strong basis for publication in high-impact journals across neuro-oncology, endocrinology, radiation oncology, and medical imaging AI.
