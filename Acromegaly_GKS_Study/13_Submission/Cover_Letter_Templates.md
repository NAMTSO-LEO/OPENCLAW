## Template 1: Neurosurgery / JNS

**[Your Name], MD, PhD**
[Institution]
[Address]
[Email] | [Phone]

[Date]

Dear Dr. [Editor Name] and Editorial Office,

Please find enclosed our manuscript titled **"Gamma Knife Radiosurgery for Cavernous Sinus–Invading Acromegaly: Multicenter Outcomes, Treatment Predictors, and Prediction Model Development and Validation"** for consideration for publication in **Neurosurgery**.

We confirm that this work has not been published previously and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere. All authors have contributed significantly to this work and have approved the final version.

**Why this paper is suitable for Neurosurgery:**

1. **Clinical relevance:** Cavernous sinus–invading acromegaly represents a surgically challenging subgroup with limited data on radiosurgical outcomes. This is the largest multicenter cohort specifically addressing this population.

2. **Novel findings:** We demonstrate that early radiosurgery, peri-procedural medication hold, and optimized dosimetry are associated with improved endocrine remission while minimizing toxicity.

3. **Methodological rigor:** We applied advanced analytical methods including propensity-based overlap weighting, multi-state modeling, and internal–external cross-validation, representing a methodological advance over prior single-center series.

4. **Clinical translation:** Validated prediction models enable individualized risk stratification to support treatment decision-making.

We believe this work will be of interest to the neurosurgical community and represents a meaningful contribution to the literature on pituitary adenoma management.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

[Your Name], MD, PhD
[Institution]

**Why this paper is suitable for Lancet Digital Health:**

This work aligns with Lancet Digital Health's focus on rigorous clinical research with demonstrated impact on patient care.

## Key Elements for All Cover Letters

| Element | What to Include |
|---------|----------------|
| **Title** | Exact manuscript title |
| **Journal** | Specific journal name |
| **Novelty** | 2-3 sentences on what makes this work unique |
| **Relevance** | Why the journal's readers should care |
| **Methods** | Brief mention of rigorous methods |
| **Ethics** | Confirmation of originality and ethics approval |
| **Conflicts** | Disclosure statement |

## Checklist Before Submission

- [ ] Manuscript title matches exactly
- [ ] Journal name is correct
- [ ] All authors listed with affiliations
- [ ] Corresponding author clearly identified
- [ ] Word count appropriate for letter (200-400 words)
- [ ] No typographical errors
- [ ] Tone is professional but enthusiastic
- [ ] Highlights novelty without overclaiming
- [ ] Mentions ethics/approval if required
