## Article 23: International Multicenter SRS (Ding 2019)

### Study Data
| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **N** | 371 patients |
| **Centers** | 10 (IGKRF) |
| **Mean follow-up** | 79 months |

### Key Results
| Outcome | Rate |
|---------|------|
| Initial remission | 54% |
| **Durable remission** | 45% |
| 10-year remission | 69% |
| Recurrence | 9% |
| New hypopituitarism | 26% |
| Cranial neuropathy | 4% |

### Critical Finding
**Medication hold** before SRS → HR=2.49 for durable remission (p=0.01)
- Stop IGF-1 lowering meds 4-8 weeks before SRS

## Article 24: IGF-1 Index + BED Study (Graffeo 2020)

### Key Results
- IGF-1i ≥2.25 → treatment failure (HR=0.51, p=0.02)
- **BED better predictor than margin dose**
- BED >200 Gy2.47 → HR=2.27 for remission (p=0.002)
- No association between BED and hypopituitarism

### Meta-Analysis
Pooled HR for IGF-1i above threshold: **2.57** (p<0.0001)
