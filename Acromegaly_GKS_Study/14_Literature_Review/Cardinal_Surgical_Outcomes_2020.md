### Study Overview

This study examines predictors of surgical remission in acromegaly patients undergoing endoscopic endonasal transsphenoidal surgery (EETS), focusing on tumor characteristics and pre/postoperative hormone levels.

---

### Key Predictors of Remission

#### Positive Findings

1. **Lower preoperative IGF-1** - Inversely correlated with remission
2. **Lower POD1 GH** - Inversely correlated with remission
3. **Smaller tumors** - GTR more likely with smaller tumors
4. **Less invasive (lower Knosp score)** - Negatively correlated with GTR
5. **Gross-total resection** - Key predictor of remission

#### Statistical Findings

- Invasive tumors significantly larger (2.1 cm vs 0.62 cm, p<0.001)
- Preoperative GH and IGF-1 positively correlated with tumor size (p=0.003 and p=0.001)
- Knosp scores negatively correlated with GTR
