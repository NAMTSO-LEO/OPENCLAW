## Suggested Discussion Paragraph

### Alignment with Consensus Guidelines

The 2025 Acromegaly Consensus Conference established that biochemical remission should be defined as IGF-1 normalization within age-stratified reference range together with GH nadir <1 ng/mL after oral glucose tolerance testing. Our study's remission criteria are fully aligned with these recommendations, incorporating both IGF-1 index ≤1 and GH criteria while patients are off medication for at least 4 weeks.

While the Consensus primarily addresses surgical and medical therapy, it acknowledges radiation therapy as an important option for patients with persistent active disease and significant adenoma burden. Our study extends this framework by specifically evaluating Gamma Knife radiosurgery outcomes, including as a primary treatment modality for cavernous sinus-invading disease where surgical complete resection is often not achievable.
