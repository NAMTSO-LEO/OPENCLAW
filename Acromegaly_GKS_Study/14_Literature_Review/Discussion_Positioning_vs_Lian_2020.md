## Suggested Discussion Paragraph

### Comparison with Fractionated Radiotherapy

Lian et al. provided important data on fractionated intensity-modulated radiotherapy (IMRT) outcomes in acromegaly, reporting a 5-year endocrine remission rate of 74.3% with a median time to remission of 36.2 months. This is comparable to reported Gamma Knife outcomes, suggesting that both fractionated and single-fraction radiotherapy achieve similar long-term biochemical control.

Importantly, their multivariate analysis identified pre-treatment IGF-1 index (IGF-1i <2.5) and younger age as significant predictors of endocrine remission. Younger patients achieved remission faster but also had higher rates of new hypopituitarism (28.3% overall, 30% at 5 years).

Our study specifically examines Gamma Knife radiosurgery outcomes, allowing direct comparison of single-fraction versus fractionated approaches in matched populations.
