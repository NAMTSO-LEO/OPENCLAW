## Suggested Discussion Paragraph

### Comparison with Surgical Outcomes

Cardinal et al. provided important data on surgical outcomes following endoscopic transsphenoidal surgery for acromegaly. In their cohort of 52 patients, 73% achieved hormonal remission with surgery alone, increasing to 87% with adjunctive medical therapy. Key predictors included lower preoperative IGF-1 levels, lower postoperative day 1 GH levels, and gross-total resection.

Notably, cavernous sinus invasion occurred in only 13% of their surgical cohort and was associated with lower rates of gross-total resection. This contrasts with our Gamma Knife cohort, which specifically focuses on cavernous sinus-invading disease - a population with inherently higher surgical complexity.

Our study provides complementary data on radiosurgical outcomes in this difficult-to-treat subgroup, where complete surgical resection is often not achievable.
