### Multivariate Analysis

| Factor | HR | P-value |
|--------|-----|---------|
| **Lower isodose line** | 1.38 | **0.001** |

**Interpretation:** For every 10% reduction in isodose line, risk of new hypopituitarism increases by **27%**.

---

### Key Findings

#### 1. Isodose Line is Key Predictor

> "Treating the target volume at an isodose line of 50% or greater and avoiding whole-sellar radiosurgery, unless necessary, will likely mitigate the risk of post-GKRS hypopituitarism."

Higher isodose line = more conformal dose = less pituitary damage.

#### 2. Functional Adenomas Have Higher Risk

- FPA (acromegaly + CD): HR = 1.510, p = 0.008
- This may relate to larger treatment volumes or prior surgery effects

#### 3. Whole-Sellar Treatment Increases Risk

- HR = 1.452, p = 0.033
- Treating entire sella increases pituitary radiation exposure

#### 4. Median Onset at 3+ Years

- 39 months median time to hypopituitarism
- Risk continues to increase through 10 years
- Emphasizes need for long-term endocrine follow-up

### Citations for Your Manuscript

**For incidence:**
> "In a multicenter cohort of 1023 patients treated with Gamma Knife radiosurgery, 24.2% developed new hypopituitarism, with actuarial rates of 22.4% at 5 years and 31.3% at 10 years."

**For timing:**
> "The median time to new hypopituitarism onset was 39 months, emphasizing the need for long-term endocrine monitoring."

**For risk factors:**
> "Cordeiro et al. identified lower isodose line as the strongest independent predictor of new hypopituitarism, with every 10% decrease associated with a 27% increased risk."

**For acromegaly specifically:**
> "Among 251 acromegaly patients in the IGKRF cohort, 96 (27.4%) developed new pituitary hormone deficiency."

### Methodological Strengths

✓ Large multicenter cohort (17 centers)
✓ Cox proportional hazards modeling
✓ Clear endpoint definitions
✓ Separate analysis by adenoma type
✓ Long follow-up (up to 246 months)

---

### Limitations

- Retrospective design
- No standardization of hormone assays across centers
- Variable follow-up protocols
- No dose-response analysis (only isodose line)
