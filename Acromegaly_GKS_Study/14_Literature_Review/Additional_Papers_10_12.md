## Article 10: Kissing Carotids - Upfront Gamma Knife

---

### Publication Details

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Title** | Acromegaly with kissing carotids - Is gamma knife radiosurgery the answer? |
| **Journal** | Journal of Clinical Neuroscience |
| **Year** | 2025 |
| **Authors** | Singh et al. |
| **Institution** | AIIMS, New Delhi |

---

### Case Summary

- **Patient**: 54-year-old male with acromegaly
- **Challenge**: "Kissing carotids" - tortuous bilateral carotid arteries in close proximity
- **Surgery**: Attempted but abandoned due to complex vascular anatomy
- **Treatment**: Primary Gamma Knife (upfront)
- **Dose**: 25 Gy at 50% isodose, tumor volume 2.05 cm³

---

### Results

| Timepoint | Tumor Volume | GH | IGF-1 |
|-----------|-------------|-----|-------|
| Pre-GKRS | 2.05 cm³ | 7.1 ng/mL | 890.6 mcg/L |
| 1 year | -13% | 2.83 ng/mL | 498 mcg/L |
| 3 years | -29% | 2.01 ng/mL | 136.4 mcg/L |

---

### Key Points

- ✅ Upfront GKS viable when surgery contraindicated
- ✅ Supports primary GKS for anatomically challenging cases
- ✅ Symptomatic improvement including claustrophobia

## Article 11: Long-Term Safety - Stroke Risk

---

### Publication Details

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Title** | Long-term safety of gamma knife radiosurgery (SRS) for acromegaly |
| **Journal** | Pituitary |
| **Year** | 2021 |
| **Pages** | 724-736 |
| **Authors** | Sims-Williams et al. |
| **Institution** | Sheffield, UK (National Centre for SRS) |

---

### Study Design

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **Total patients** | 118 |
| **Analyzed** | 104 |
| **Mean follow-up** | 134 months (median 107) |
| **Median dose** | 30 Gy |
| **CS invasion** | 81% |

---

### Key Results

#### Stroke Risk
- **No excess stroke** compared to normal population
- **SIR = 1.36** (95% CI 0.27-3.96) vs Oxford study
- **SIR = 0.52** (95% CI 0.06-1.89) vs Copenhagen study

#### Visual Complications (MRI-guided SRS only, n=68)
- **No visual acuity loss**
- **3% ophthalmoplegia**

#### Repeat Radiation Effects
- >1 radiation treatment associated with **increased** ophthalmoplegia and visual worsening

---

### Conclusion

> "Stroke rate is not increased by SRS for acromegaly. Accurate MRI-based treatment planning and single SRS treatment allow the lowest complication rates."

## Article 12: Low-Dose GKRS (Full Paper)

---

### Publication Details

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Title** | Low-Dose Gamma Knife Radiosurgery for Acromegaly |
| **Journal** | Neurosurgery |
| **Year** | 2019 |
| **Authors** | Pai et al. |
| **Institution** | Taipei Veterans General Hospital |

---

### Key Data

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| **N** | 76 patients |
| **Median margin dose** | 15.8 Gy (range 11.9-22) |
| **Median isodose** | 57.5% |
| **Median tumor volume** | 2.75 mL |
| **CS invasion** | 51.3% |

---

### Outcomes

#### Remission

| Timepoint | Rate |
|-----------|------|
| **Overall** | 43.4% (33/76) |
| 4 years | 20.3% |
| 8 years | 49.9% |
| **12 years** | **76.3%** |

> Key insight: Slow but cumulative - 12-year rate higher than typical high-dose studies

#### Multivariate Predictors

| Factor | HR | P-value |
|--------|-----|---------|
| **No CS invasion** | 0.339 | 0.005 |
| Lower IGF-1 (per ng/mL) | 0.998 | 0.026 |
| Lower GH (per ng/mL) | 0.971 | 0.036 |
| No medical therapy at GKRS | 4.595 | - |

#### Hypopituitarism

| Timepoint | Rate |
|-----------|------|
| **Overall** | 11.8% (9/76) |
| 4 years | 3% |
| 8 years | 14% |
| 10 years | 22.2% |

- Median time to hypopituitarism: **83.6 months (~7 years)**

#### Tumor Control: 98.7%

---

### Key Insight: Dose NOT Linear

- ≥18 Gy vs <18 Gy: **Not significant** (p=0.062)
- Lower dose works but more slowly

## Consolidated Evidence Summary

### Dose-Response Relationship

| Study | Dose | Remission | Time |
|-------|------|-----------|------|
| Standard | 25-30 Gy | 50-60% | 2-3 years |
| Low-dose | 15-18 Gy | 43-76% | 8-12 years |

**Conclusion**: Lower dose works but much slower; long-term rates similar

### CS Invasion Effect

All studies consistently show CS invasion is the strongest negative predictor:
- Surgery: r = -0.953
- GKS: HR ~0.34-0.64
- Low-dose: HR = 0.34 (p=0.005)

### Long-term Safety

- Hypopituitarism: 10-30% by 10 years (cumulative)
- Stroke: No increased risk (Sims-Williams)
- Visual: <3% with modern planning
