### Background
Acromegaly due to cavernous sinus–invading pituitary adenomas represents a challenging subtype with limited data on outcomes after Gamma Knife stereotactic radiosurgery. We aimed to characterize endocrine remission, predictors, and treatment strategy effects in a multicenter cohort.

### Methods
We conducted a retrospective international multicenter cohort study of patients with cavernous sinus–invading acromegaly undergoing Gamma Knife radiosurgery. Primary endpoint was time to endocrine remission (IGF-1 index ≤1.0, off medication). Secondary endpoints included biochemical recurrence, radiographic progression, new hypopituitarism, and salvage treatment.

We used Kaplan–Meier methods for survival analysis, multivariable Cox proportional hazards regression with restricted cubic splines for nonlinear associations, and frailty terms for center heterogeneity. Treatment strategy comparisons (early vs delayed radiosurgery, targeted vs whole-sella planning, medication hold vs no hold) were performed using overlap weighting based on propensity scores.
