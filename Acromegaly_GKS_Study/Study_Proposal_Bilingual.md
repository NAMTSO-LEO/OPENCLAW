## Background and Research Hypothesis

### Background

**English:**

Acromegaly is caused by growth hormone–secreting pituitary adenomas and remains associated with substantial cardiovascular, metabolic, musculoskeletal, and neuroendocrine morbidity if not adequately controlled. Although transsphenoidal surgery is the standard first-line treatment, biochemical remission declines markedly when the tumor invades the cavernous sinus, as cavernous sinus extension substantially increases surgical complexity and operative risk, thereby making gross-total resection significantly more difficult to achieve.

**中文:**

肢端肥大症由分泌生长激素的垂体腺瘤引起，如未得到充分控制，仍与显著的心血管、代谢、肌肉骨骼及神经内分泌相关并发症有关。尽管经蝶窦手术是标准的一线治疗，但当肿瘤侵犯海绵窦时，生化缓解率会明显下降，因为海绵窦扩展会显著增加手术复杂性和手术风险，从而使全切除更难实现。

---

### Gamma Knife Radiosurgery (GKS)

**English:**

Gamma Knife radiosurgery (GKS) is widely used for persistent, residual, or recurrent acromegaly, particularly when adenomatous remnants are confined to the cavernous sinus. Existing single-center studies, international multicenter cohorts, and meta-analyses consistently demonstrate excellent radiographic tumor control but slower and more heterogeneous endocrine remission, often occurring over years rather than months. Prior work has also suggested that baseline hormonal burden, timing from surgery to radiosurgery, peri-radiosurgical medication management, coverage strategy, and radiosurgical planning variables influence endocrine outcomes.

**中文:**

伽玛刀放射外科（GKS）广泛用于持续性、残余性或复发性肢端肥大症，尤其是在腺瘤残余局限于海绵窦时。现有单中心研究、国际多中心队列研究及荟萃分析一致表明，其影像学肿瘤控制效果优异，但内分泌缓解较慢且异质性较大，往往需要数年而非数月。既往研究还提示，基线激素负荷、从手术到放射外科的时间间隔、围放射外科期药物管理、照射覆盖策略以及放射外科计划变量均会影响内分泌结局。

---

### Research Gap

**English:**

Despite these advances, the current literature remains fragmented. Most studies include mixed acromegaly populations rather than a dedicated cohort of cavernous sinus–invading disease. A focused international analysis of cavernous sinus–invading acromegaly is therefore needed.

**中文:**

尽管已有这些进展，当前文献仍较为零散。大多数研究纳入的是混合型肢端肥大症人群，而非专门针对海绵窦侵犯型疾病的队列。因此，有必要开展一项聚焦海绵窦侵犯型肢端肥大症的国际性分析。

---

### Hypothesis

**English:**

We hypothesize that GKS provides high long-term tumor control and clinically meaningful endocrine remission in patients with cavernous sinus–invading acromegaly, but that durable remission and toxicity are strongly influenced by baseline hormonal burden, residual cavernous sinus tumor volume and location, timing from surgery to radiosurgery, peri-radiosurgical medication status, and radiosurgical planning variables.

**中文:**

我们假设，GKS可为海绵窦侵犯型肢端肥大症患者提供较高的长期肿瘤控制率和具有临床意义的内分泌缓解，但持久缓解及毒性反应会受到基线激素负荷，海绵窦残余肿瘤体积和位置、从手术到放射外科的时间间隔、围放射外科期药物状态以及放射外科计划变量的显著影响。
