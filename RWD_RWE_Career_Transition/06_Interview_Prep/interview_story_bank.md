# Interview Story Bank

## 1. Why are you moving from traditional trials into RWD/RWE?
Draft answer:
I have a strong foundation in statistical analysis from traditional clinical research, and I want to apply that rigor to questions that are increasingly answered through real-world data. RWD/RWE is attractive to me because it connects methodology with practical clinical and business decisions, including treatment patterns, comparative effectiveness, safety, and patient outcomes in routine care.

## 2. What is your advantage coming from a clinical trial background?
Draft answer:
My trial background gives me a strong grounding in study design, endpoint thinking, bias awareness, and analytical discipline. That foundation is highly valuable in RWE, where methodological rigor is even more important because the data are less controlled than in randomized settings.

## 3. What do you need to strengthen for RWE roles?
Draft answer:
I am intentionally deepening my expertise in observational study methods, causal inference, and the structure and limitations of claims, EHR, and registry data, while translating my existing statistical experience into real-world evidence applications.
