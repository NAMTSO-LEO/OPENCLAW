# 90-Day RWD/RWE Transition Plan

## Goal
Move from traditional clinical trial/statistics positioning into a credible RWD/RWE analyst profile.

## Days 1–30: Reframe and Position
### Objectives
- Clarify target roles
- Reframe current experience into RWE language
- Build core story for resume, LinkedIn, and interviews

### Tasks
- Update resume for RWD/RWE positions
- Update LinkedIn headline and About section
- Create a 1-minute and 3-minute transition story
- List 20 target companies and 20 target job titles
- Identify key gaps: causal inference, claims/EHR familiarity, RWE vocabulary

### Output
- 1 tailored resume draft
- 1 LinkedIn draft
- 1 interview story bank

## Days 31–60: Build Credibility
### Objectives
- Show evidence of RWE readiness
- Build portfolio-style materials
- Strengthen technical and conceptual language

### Tasks
- Create 2 mock RWE case studies
  - treatment pattern study idea
  - comparative effectiveness or safety study idea
- Write 1 short RWE methods note each week
- Build a cheat sheet for:
  - propensity score methods
  - matching vs weighting
  - confounding and bias in observational data
  - claims vs EHR vs registry data
- Practice explaining RCT vs RWE differences in interview style

### Output
- 2 portfolio case studies
- 3–4 concept notes
- 1 methods cheat sheet

## Days 61–90: Apply and Network
### Objectives
- Start applying strategically
- Build networking momentum
- Improve interview confidence

### Tasks
- Apply to 5–10 high-fit roles per week
- Reach out to 2–3 professionals per week in RWE/HEOR/pharma analytics
- Practice 10 common interview questions
- Customize resume per role family
- Track applications and responses

### Output
- Active application tracker
- Networking message templates
- Refined interview answers

## Success markers by Day 90
- Resume clearly positioned for RWD/RWE roles
- LinkedIn profile supports transition story
- At least 2 portfolio artifacts completed
- Can confidently explain observational methods at a high level
- Applications submitted to target employers
- Interview-ready narrative for transition from trials to RWE
