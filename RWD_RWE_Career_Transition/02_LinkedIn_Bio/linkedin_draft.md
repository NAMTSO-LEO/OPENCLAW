I am particularly interested in observational study design, confounding control, healthcare data interpretation, and using data to generate actionable evidence for medical, clinical, and strategic teams.

Currently, I am focused on strengthening my practical RWE toolkit across claims/EHR-style thinking, causal inference concepts, and portfolio-building in real-world analytics.
