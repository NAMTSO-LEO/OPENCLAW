#### Critical Sentences (Must Include)
1. "clinically distinct and surgically challenging subgroup"
2. "was associated with a higher likelihood of"
3. "After overlap weighting, covariate balance was achieved"
4. "Findings should be interpreted cautiously"
5. "Machine learning did not substantially outperform regression-based models"
6. "consistent performance across centers"
7. "individualized treatment decision-making"

#### Forbidden Language
- ❌ "improved" → use "associated with"
- ❌ "better" / "superior" → use "higher likelihood"
- ❌ "definitive" / "novel" → avoid overclaiming

#### Second PDF: Wu et al. (Clinical Neurology and Neurosurgery 2021)

**Paper:** "Comparing primary gamma knife radiosurgery and postoperative gamma knife radiosurgery for acromegaly"

**Key Details:**
- N=75 (38 primary GKS, 37 postoperative GKS)
- West China Hospital, Sichuan University
- No significant difference in remission: 21.05% vs 21.62% (p=0.831)
- 8-year actuarial remission: 70.6% vs 78.8% (p=0.800)
- Only predictor: nadir GH after OGTT (HR=0.637, p=0.039)
